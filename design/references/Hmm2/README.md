## Acceptance rules

- Compare the rendered Android screen against its matching original at the same landscape aspect ratio.
- Preserve the original content hierarchy, positions, proportions, colors, typography, imagery, borders, and control states.
- Keep every control functional; a screenshot pasted over the app is not an implementation.
- Do not change the Home screen as part of this reference-driven work.
- Do not claim a screen is an exact match until a rendered build has been visually compared with its reference.
